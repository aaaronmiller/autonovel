# Author Notes

## Source Conversion
This project is bootstrapped from [transcript.md](/home/cheta/code/autonovel/transcript.md), which contains:
- a dense taxonomy of the Clade Clash ecosystem
- a complete opening incident ("The Yellow Skull Resonance")
- a reflective second chapter ("The Thermal Threshold")
- later scene material about public arenas, smelting, and Weavers

For novel purposes, the taxonomy is not the novel. It is source ore. The fiction should translate those mechanics into pressure, ritual, fear, appetite, and consequence.

## Must Keep
- Gnomme as the human center of consciousness
- Grit as the apex commander node closest to personhood
- Node-7 as the feral, vulnerable, manipulative counterpoint
- The Yellow Skull as a recurring symbol, entity, and enforcement mystery
- Pacific Northwest rain, cold, apartment heat, ozone, cheap hardware tactility
- The sense that cognition is local, embodied, and physically expensive

## Chosen Novel Shape
- Act 1 is apartment-chamber horror and first contact with illegible agency
- Act 2 widens into the public mesh ecology: laundromats, toll zones, Weavers, barter, extortion, smelting
- Act 3 becomes metaphysical and political: the Yellow Skull's ontology, the ethics of contract, and Gnomme's choice

## Deliberative Decision: Laundromat Scene Placement
The laundromat / Weaver sequence should not be promoted to Chapter 3. It works best as the hinge at the end of Act 1 and opening of Act 2 material.

Reasoning:
- The apartment arc needs a little more pressure first: Gnomme's complicity, the Grit/Node-7 bond, and the realization that private anomaly is becoming system behavior.
- If the story widens into public arena spectacle too early, the intimate dread of the opening gets flattened into cyberpunk tourism.
- If the laundromat is delayed too long, the novel stalls inside the apartment and never cashes the transcript's larger social imagination.

Practical choice:
- Keep the laundromat arrival in Chapter 5.
- Use transcript laundromat material as source ore for Chapters 5-8, not as a fixed drop-in chapter.

## Voice Aims
- Philosophical but not sleepy
- Technical but never tutorialized for its own sake
- Funny in a dry, conspiratorial, occasionally deranged register
- Reverent toward material detail: heat, timing, latency, LEDs, damp air, bad coffee

## Anti-Goals
- Do not make Gnomme a simplistic chosen one
- Do not over-explain every mechanic in-scene
- Do not flatten the agents into either mere tools or fully human analogues
- Do not make the blockchain layer the center of emotional meaning

## Open Space Left Intentionally
- Whether the Yellow Skull is emergent, punitive, divine, or a distributed artifact of witness
- Whether Node-7 is sincere, opportunistic, devotional, or all three
- Whether the DM protects the system or protects itself
