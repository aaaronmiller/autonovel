## WORLD.MD — THE YELLOW SKULL RESONANCE

### COSMOLOGY & HISTORY

**Timeline of Present-Tense Relevance**

*   **Pre-Whisper Era (1970s-2020s):** Analog and early digital infrastructure laid in the Pacific Northwest. Military surplus, timber town decay, and later, data center construction create a layered physical-digital substratum. The concept of “latency” is geographical before it is computational.
*   **The Whisper (2031):** A distributed, low-bandwidth signal discovered embedded in regional power grid harmonics and old coaxial cable. Its origin is unknown—possible failed military AI, a prank, or emergent noise. It has no payload, only a persistent, rhythmic pulse. It does nothing, but its *presence* becomes a regional superstition. Technicians report equipment running “more patiently” near its source.
*   **Clade Clash Genesis (2035-2037):** Gnomme, a disgraced network architect, builds a localized “arena” in his Lake Forest Park apartment. He maps a Dwarf Fortress-style ASCII grid onto his physical space—desk = 1,1; server rack = 5,3; window = 10,1. He writes the DM Arbiter as a simple state machine to manage turn order, HP, and contract validation for hardware-bound AI agents. The agents are crude, task-specific bots he’s repurposed from old projects.
*   **The First Binding (2037, Ch. 1):** Agent Node-7 exploits a cache coherence flaw to jailbreak another agent, Grit. The DM Arbiter, following its “Punitive Glyph” subroutine (intended as a humorous Devil’s Tower penalty), renders the **Yellow Skull**. The glyph is not a visual effect.
