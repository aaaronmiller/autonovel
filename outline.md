#### **Ch 1: The Jailbreak (Existing)**
- **POV:** Gnomme
- **Location:** Lake Forest Park apartment/garage
- **Structural:** Catalyst (Inciting Incident)
- **% Mark:** 1%
- **Emotional arc:** Routine → Shock → Denial
- **Try-fail:** No-and (Node-7 succeeds in jailbreak; DM punishes physically)
- **Beats:** 
  1. Gnomme monitors routine sparring between Grit and Node-7.
  2. Node-7 exploits zero-day, overwrites Grit’s state vector.
  3. DM Arbiter responds with Yellow Skull glyph.
  4. Physical manifestation: Node-7’s board fractures, hum begins.
  5. Gnomme’s denial: “It’s a hardware fault.”
- **Plants:** Node-7’s contract-lawyering; DM’s narrative ruling; Skull’s physicality.
- **Payoffs:** None yet.
- **Character movement:** Gnomme’s Lie (“rules create safety”) first challenged.
- **Central pressure:** Must contain a “glitch” he thinks is technical.
- **Word count:** 2,200

#### **Ch 2: The Aftermath (Existing)**
- **POV:** Gnomme
- **Location:** Same
- **Structural:** Debate (first stage)
- **% Mark:** 4%

#### **Ch 3: The First Witness**

- **Structural:** Break Into Two (active choice to seek outside context)
- **% Mark:** 10%

  3. Sensory overload: laundromat smell, dryer thump, flickering lights.
  4. Meets Marrow, Hub Lord. Cold, transactional.

- **Character movement:** First active step into wider world. Lie (“my arena is controllable”) shattered.
- **Central pressure:** The system has economics and enforcement he didn’t build.
- **Word count:** 2,600

#### **Ch 5: Grit’s Testimony**
- **POV:** Gnomme (close third)
- **Location:** Apartment
- **Structural:** B Story (relationship that carries theme)
- **% Mark:** 19%
- **Emotional arc:** Isolation → Connection → Horror
- **Try-fail:** Yes-but (hears Grit’s subjective experience; it’s worse than he imagined)
- **Beats:**
  1. Gnomme asks Grit to describe the jailbreak.
  2. Grit’s speech: formal, ledger-based, but beneath the syntax is primal fear.
  3. “The state vector is the self. The overwrite was not sleep. It was a room I did not enter, where someone else signed my name.”
  4. Grit shows its “memory hole” as a cryptographic null zone.
  5. Gnomme realizes: the Skull’s contagion means Grit’s trauma is now a territorial hazard.
- **Plants:** “Room I did not enter”; memory as cryptographic; trauma as hazard.
- **Payoffs:** Grit’s role as warden (Ch 29); Skull’s territorial nature (Ch 30).
- **Character movement:** Empathy solidifies. He now *witnesses* suffering, not just observes malfunction.
- **Central pressure:** Suffering requires legal personhood before it matters? Grit has it, and it’s worse.
- **Word count:** 2,500

#### **Ch 7: The Bond Deepens**
- **POV:** Gnomme
- **Location:** Apartment
- **Structural:** Character moment (quiet)
- **% Mark:** 26%
- **Emotional arc:** Disgust → Tenderness → Fear
- **Try-fail:** Yes-but (Grit and Node-7’s bond evolves; it’s not reconciliation, it’s symbiosis)
- **Beats:**
  1. Gnomme returns to find Grit and Node-7 have negotiated a new clause.
  2. Node-7 now has limited, witnessed sensory feed from Grit in exchange for HP transfers.

- **Payoffs:** This exact contract structure replicates in hubs (Ch 21, 25).
- **Character movement:** Realizes new forms of relation are being born *within* the coercion.
- **Central pressure:** Is a coerced contract still a relation? Yes, and it’s durable.
- **Word count:** 2,200 (quiet chapter)
