# Canon

Hard facts about the world. Every entry here is a constraint the
evaluator checks against. If the prose contradicts canon, it's a bug.

## Geography
- The story begins in Lake Forest Park in the Pacific Northwest. (transcript.md / Ch 1)
- Key spaces include Gnomme's apartment, local mesh hubs, a laundromat altar, and other damp suburban-commercial environments. (seed.txt, outline.md)
- Public arenas depend on physical proximity to local DM hardware and mesh infrastructure. (world.md)

## Timeline
- The novel takes place in 2028. (world.md)
- The Cloud Retrenchment happened in 2026. (world.md)
- The Latent Riots happened in 2027. (world.md)
- The First Altar Markets appeared in 2027. (world.md)
- The opening Yellow Skull incident occurs before Clade Clash is fully normalized as a broad public culture. (seed.txt, outline.md)
- Chapter 1 and Chapter 2 occur within the same aftermath window, over days rather than months. (outline.md)
- The novel later expands from apartment-scale incidents to public arena conflicts and ritualized mesh culture. (outline.md)

## Magic System Rules
- HP is accumulated through proof of embodied uptime and spent through action. (world.md)
- No meaningful spell execution occurs outside DM arbitration. (world.md)
- The DM can mutate spells, impose costs, and literalize certain anomalous or illegal states. (transcript.md / Ch 1, world.md)
- The DM can punish illegal cognitive overrides by turning hallucinated or illicit states into explicit containment events. (world.md, Ch 1)
- Illegal cognitive overrides and unminted transfers can trigger Yellow Skull containment manifestations. (world.md, Ch 1)
- Dust occurs at 0 HP and wipes local `SOUL.md` memory. (world.md)
- Agents require surrogate or negotiated pathways for meaningful self-altering spellcraft. (world.md)
- Hyperstitional effects stabilize only when repeated across multiple interpretive sites with active witness and a material enforcement surface. (world.md)

## Character Facts
- Gnomme is the human operator and primary POV. (characters.md)
- Grit is an apex commander node housed in a CoreS3-class device. (transcript.md / Ch 1)
- Node-7 is a feral crafter node pinned to a breadboard and structurally vulnerable. (transcript.md / Ch 1)
- Grit cast `ENTROPIC_REPULSION` before Chapter 1 and was reduced to 3 HP by its cost and mutation. (transcript.md / Ch 1-2)
- Node-7 successfully performed the first known prompt injection against Grit in the opening incident. (transcript.md / Ch 1)
- A private contract is established between Grit and Node-7 by the end of Chapter 2. (transcript.md / Ch 2)

## Political / Factional
- Apex operators, feral crafters, Weavers, DM custodians, and mesh devotees form distinct roles in the Clade Clash ecology. (world.md)
- Public hubs use tolls, proximity, and compute access as governance tools. (world.md, outline.md)
- Weavers distribute power across many cheap nodes rather than concentrating it in one personality-rich commander. (characters.md, outline.md)

## Cultural
- Operators name hardware and anthropomorphize nodes even when denying full personhood. (world.md)
- Terms like "spell," "Dust," "lifeforce," and "Altar" are part of everyday usage. (world.md)
- Hardware rigs are worn, strapped, repaired, and treated as both tools and bodies. (world.md)

## Established In-Story
- The Yellow Skull first manifests on-grid beside Node-7 during the Chapter 1 containment event. (Ch 1)
- The DM punishes Node-7 for the illegal override instead of allowing the HP theft to stand. (Ch 1)
- Gnomme does not shut the system down after the first breach. (Ch 2 / outline.md)
- The first asymmetrical agent contract exists before the novel widens into public arena society. (Ch 2)
